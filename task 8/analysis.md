# VPN Benefits and Limitations

## Benefits

- **Privacy Protection**: Hides your IP and location.
- **Public Wi-Fi Security**: Encrypts traffic on open networks.
- **Access Restricted Content**: Allows bypassing geo-blocks.
- **Anonymity**: Prevents ISP and trackers from monitoring behavior.

## Limitations

- **Speed Reduction**: Encrypted traffic may be slower.
- **Limited Free Servers**: Free VPNs offer fewer location options.
- **Logging Policies**: Some providers may log usage despite claims.
- **Blocked Services**: Some websites block known VPN IPs.

## Example VPN Used

- **Service**: ProtonVPN (Free Tier)
- **Encryption**: AES-256, OpenVPN/IKEv2 protocols
- **No-logs Policy**: Yes
